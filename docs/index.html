How to play
  • Win by building 4 outposts (5 with two players, 4 otherwise). Two-player games close two corners.
  • Start of turn: draw up to 2 cards. Then mine: each planet with 2+ of your troops yields 1 crystal or 1 ore.
  • Spend 1 crystal per troop. New troops arrive at your home station.
  • You get 2 movements. A movement sends any number of troops one lane. The warp gate costs 1 crystal and jumps between any two of the four edge planets.
  • Troops never enter a home station, including their own.
  • Moving onto rivals starts a battle: each side rolls one die, high roll wins, defender wins ties. The loser removes one troop. Repeat until one side is gone.
  • Build an outpost on a planet where you have 3+ troops for 3 ore. Only one outpost per commander per planet; rivals can share planets.
  • End of turn: get 1 troop plus 1 per outpost. You may recycle one card.
